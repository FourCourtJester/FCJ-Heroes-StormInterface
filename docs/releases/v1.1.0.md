## Interface

- **Colour-blind mode reaches our art.** On Blizzard's colour-blind option, blue becomes cyan and red becomes orange (Ahli's colours) across the top plate, the core bars, the hero plates, Ctrl+1's talent squares, the inspect card's edge, and the stat panes' bands, row stripes, totals and numbers.
- **HUD keys.** Ctrl+E hides everything, the top plate included; Ctrl+Shift+E hides all but the top plate. Nothing bleeds through.
- **The game's end** hides the lower HUD, so no heal pop-up is left over the victory splash.
- **Ctrl+4 is XP Contribution.** Blizzard's XP breakdown by team - each team's total and its XP from heroes, mercenaries, minions, passive and structures - which observers never get. Siege and XP join healing on Ctrl+3.
- **The pause box** moves off the logo plate and clock, to sit 40 px under the centre banners.
- **Alterac Pass.** Keep shields on our core bars: three pips outside the bar's border, emptying as each keep falls. The cavalry banner sits 25 px higher, clear of the bars.
- **Rexxar.** A dead Rexxar darkens like every other dead plate, his timer back on the plate's centre.
