## Interface

- **Team XP is back in the top bar.** Blizzard's own XP bars now sit along the bottom of the top bar's wings, under each team's level. They are cut to the wings' slants and tucked under their rim, and they fill outward from the hexagon.
- **The talent lead in gold.** While a team is a talent tier ahead, its XP bar turns gold, and a glint sweeps along the gold from the hexagon outward every 6 seconds. Blizzard's own flame stays behind the leading team's level.
- **Leaner hero rows.** The XP strip over the hero plates is gone. The core bars sit straight on the plates, and the row's frame runs round the plates alone.
- **Level digits re-centred** in the wings, above the new XP bars.
- **The inspect card opens clean.** After it closes, it no longer flashes the last unit's card, bars or command card on its next open. It now sits a few pixels lower, following the hero row.
- **Ctrl+Shift+H hides the heal pop-ups.**
- **On fire on shared plates.** Rexxar and Misha's plate and the Vikings' plate now get the on-fire flames round them, like every other hero.
